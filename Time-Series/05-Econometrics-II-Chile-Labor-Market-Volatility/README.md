# Econometrics II - Chile Labor Market and Macro Volatility
This project analyzes how macroeconomic shocks and expectations are transmitted to unemployment and employment in Chile between 2010 and 2021.
The analysis builds a counterfactual path for the labor force participation rate around the COVID-19 disruption using ARIMA models. It also studies the dynamic interaction between labor market variables and business expectations through a multivariate framework, including VAR models, Granger causality tests, impulse response functions, and variance decomposition. Finally, it incorporates a macro-financial block by modeling CLP/USD exchange rate volatility using a GARCH(1,1) approach.
## Objective
The objective is to evaluate how macroeconomic uncertainty, expectations, and exchange rate volatility relate to labor market outcomes in Chile.
